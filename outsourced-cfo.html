Outsourced CFO

Strategic Financial Leadership, Without the Full-Time Cost.

HypeFi embeds as your fractional CFO — bringing institutional-grade financial strategy, investor-ready reporting, and hands-on capital advisory to early-stage and growth-stage companies at a fraction of the cost of an in-house hire.

Core Services

What We Do

End-to-end financial infrastructure for companies building toward their next raise or inflection point.

Financial Modeling

Investor-grade 3-statement models, scenario analysis, unit economics, and cohort analysis — built to withstand diligence and inform strategic decisions.

Budgeting & Forecasting

Rolling 12–24 month forecasts, departmental budgeting, and variance analysis to keep operations aligned with your capital plan.

Fundraising Strategy

End-to-end fundraising support — from narrative development and data room preparation to investor targeting and term sheet evaluation.

Investor Reporting

Monthly investor updates, board package preparation, KPI dashboards, and cap table management that keep stakeholders informed and confident.

Capital Structure Advisory

Debt vs. equity analysis, SAFE/convertible note structuring, dilution modeling, and pre-money/post-money scenario planning before you sign anything.

Due Diligence Support

Financial diligence preparation, data room organization, quality of earnings analysis, and real-time support during investor or acquirer diligence processes.

Strategic Finance Advisory

Pricing strategy, expansion modeling, M&A evaluation, and board-level financial guidance to support high-stakes operational and strategic decisions.

Accounting & Controls Setup

Chart of accounts structuring, accounting system selection, internal controls design, and month-end close processes — the infrastructure your investors expect.

KPI & Metrics Framework

Define and track the metrics that matter most at your stage — CAC, LTV, NRR, burn multiple, and sector-specific benchmarks that tell the right story.
