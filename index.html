Tech Stack

Structure for Seamless Growth

Most early finance stacks get assembled one tool at a time — a bank feed here, a payroll app there — and nobody owns how they fit together. So the books close late, the numbers disagree, and the forecast lives in a spreadsheet someone rebuilds every month.

We design the stack around how you will actually run the company: books that close on schedule, payroll and spend that post themselves, and reporting that reads straight off the ledger.

  • Scalable accounting system setup
  • Chart of accounts built for your business model
  • Bank, card and spend integrations
  • Payroll and HR systems
  • A month-end close that runs on schedule
  • Monthly investor-ready financials
  • Dashboards and KPIs fed from the books
